Transportation Guide

From Macau | HongKong | Zhuhai

Macau to University of Macau, N1 Building

Map

MAP of University of Macau, N1 Building

By Taxi
  • Calling Taxi by Phone: +853 2828 3283 / +853 288 12345
  • Taxi Mobile APP: Android, iOS
  • Approximate price from below locations to University of Macau, N1 Building:
    • From Gongbei Port: 15 km, 25 min, 140 MOP (approximate).
    • From Hengqin Port: 3 km, 10 min, 45 MOP (approximate).
    • From HZMB Macau Port: 16 km, 30 min, 150 MOP (approximate).
    • From Macau Taipa Ferry Terminal: 7.8 km, 15 min, 80 MOP (approximate).
  • Waiting and luggage will be charged additionally.
  • Prepare some cash for taxi (100 MOP ≈ 90 RMB).
  • If you do not have cash, you can also pay with WeChat/Alipay but the currency rate will be 1:1:1 for RMB:HKD:MOP.
By Bus
  • Bus information
  • Real Time Bus location
  • Macao Bus (巴士報站) APP: Android, iOS
  • From Gongbei Port: Take 51A or AP1 to AV. 1º DE MAIO and transfer to 73, off at UM / UNIVERSITY HALL
  • From Hengqin Port: Take 701X to UM
  • From HZMB Macau Port: Take 102X to ROTUNDA MARGINAL / ZONAS ECOLÓGICAS and transfer to 71S, 71, 72 or 73, off at UM / UNIVERSITY HALL
  • From Macau Taipa Ferry Terminal: Take 51A to AV. CIDADE NOVA / VENETIAN and transfer to 72, off at UM / UNIVERSITY HALL

Hong Kong to Macau

Hong Kong International Airport –> Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Macao Port
  • Hong Kong International Airport Buses to Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge (HZMB) Macao Port
  • OR
  • Take B4 Bus from Hong Kong International Airport to HZMB Hong Kong Port –> Take HZMBus to HZMB Macao Port
Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Hong Kong Port –> Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Macao Port
  • Take HZMBus to HZMB Macao Port
Hong Kong Macau Ferry Terminal –> Macau Outer Harbour Ferry Terminal / Macau Taipa Ferry Terminal
  • Take ferry from Hong Kong Macau Ferry Terminal to Macau Outer Harbour Ferry Terminal / Macau Taipa Ferry Terminal. Cotai Water Jet and Turbo Jet provide the ferry services schedules.

Zhuhai to Macau

Zhuhai Airport
  • Take shuttle Buses (拱北A线) to Gongbei port
  • OR
  • Take shuttle Buses (横琴长隆线) to Hengqin port
  • OR
  • Take Taxi / DiDi to Gongbei port / Hengqin port
